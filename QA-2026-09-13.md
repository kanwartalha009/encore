## 2. Found, NOT fixed — needs your call (Feature Rule)

**A. Back-in-stock and preorder emails do not send out of the box.** Notifications → Provider defaults to **Off — "No automated customer emails"**; the only other options are Klaviyo or Shopify Flow. Railway log 16:13:59: `[back-in-stock] dispatch FAILED … no_channel: choose Klaviyo or Shopify Flow`. So a merchant who installs Encore, puts Notify-me on a product and collects signups gets **nothing delivered** unless they run Klaviyo or build a Flow. Every competitor (Notify Me!, Appikon, STOQ, Timesact) sends the restock and preorder emails itself. The transport already exists (`email.server.ts`, used only by the Flow action).
- *What:* third provider **"Encore email (built-in)"** — sends via `email.server.ts` directly; becomes the default when `ENCORE_EMAIL_API_KEY`/`FROM` are set.
- *Why:* the app's core promise ("notify customers the moment inventory returns") is silently unmet on a default install; this is the #1 go-to-market blocker I found.
- *Scope:* one provider option + ~40 lines in `waitlist-notify.server.ts` / `notify-events.server.ts` calling `sendEmail()` with the existing templates; unsubscribe link + footer already templated.
- *Risk:* deliverability is ours (sender domain per store or a shared `notify@encore…` with the store name as display name). Mitigation: require a verified `ENCORE_EMAIL_FROM`; per-store custom sender later.

**B. Theme-integration selector overrides** (custom themes) — proposal already in `AUDIT-CONFIG-2026-09-13.md` §7.

**C. Insights → Demand tab** shows one number in an otherwise empty card with "Open full view". Not a bug; it reads unfinished. Proposal: render the top-5 demand rows inline (data already loaded by `/app/demand`).
